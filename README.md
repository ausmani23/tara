# Tara

A personal exercise timer, live at **https://ausmani23.github.io/tara/**

## Putting it on your phone

1. Open that link in **Safari** (iPhone) or Chrome (Android).
2. Tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**.

That's it. It then behaves like any other app: full screen, works with no
signal, keeps the screen awake while a routine is running. Nothing to install
and no account to make.

## What's in it

**Today** is the day's plan. **Upcoming** is the next ten days — drag a session
by its handle to move it to a different day if life gets in the way. **Browse**
is everything at once, ignoring the calendar, for when you want something that
isn't scheduled.

Every day has the same two ten-minute morning routines and whatever the block
has programmed for that day. Routines run themselves: timed moves count down
and advance on their own, rep-based moves wait for you to tap.

## Once a week

Tap **Notes & export → Copy everything** and send it to Adaner. That's the
whole hand-off — the app has no server, so it's the only way what you actually
did reaches next week's programming. Add notes during the week whenever
something is worth saying: what hurt, what felt easy, what you skipped and why.

The **morning check-in** takes ten seconds and matters more than it looks.
First-step foot pain in particular moves weeks before anything else does, so
it's the earliest signal that the loading is or isn't working.

## The block

Five weeks, 11 August to 15 September 2026, aimed at one timed mile at the end
of it. Two lifting days, three running days, classes, and a mile test on the
last day and not before.
